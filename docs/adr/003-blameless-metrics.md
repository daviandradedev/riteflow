# ADR 003 - Blameless metrics policy

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Comparative dashboards often become person scoreboards, which destroys psychological safety and contradicts the original retrospective brief.

## Decision

Across-time comparison and Loop verification use **system metrics only**: unique items, done/open, aging open, bottleneck share, carryover rate, new vs carried scope. Contributor mix may appear inside a single snapshot as a collective alphabetical view, never as a ranked trend across sprints.

## Consequences

Compare and Loop UIs omit person series. Copy states the policy in both languages.
